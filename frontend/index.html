About PSTU EC

The PSTU Entrepreneurship Club is a dynamic platform that fosters innovation, creativity, and business acumen among students. We provide resources, mentorship, and networking opportunities to help turn ideas into viable businesses.

Vision

To create a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship at PSTU

Mission
