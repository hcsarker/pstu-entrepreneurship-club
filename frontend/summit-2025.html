Highlights

The PSTU Entrepreneurship Summit 2025 brought together student founders, alumni entrepreneurs, investors and mentors for a day of talks, panels, and networking. The summit focused on ideation, fundraising, and sustainable growth in early-stage startups.

  • Keynotes on founder journeys and product-market fit
  • Panel: Fundraising in Bangladesh – realities and myths
  • Startup showcase featuring 10 student teams
  • Investor/mentor open networking
