About PSTU Entrepreneurship Club

Empowering the next generation of innovators and business leaders

Our Mission

To foster an entrepreneurial mindset among PSTU students by providing resources, mentorship, and opportunities to develop and launch innovative business ideas.

  • Promote innovation and creativity
  • Provide practical business skills
  • Connect students with industry leaders
  • Support startup development

Our Vision

To establish PSTU as a hub for entrepreneurial excellence in Bangladesh, producing graduates who create jobs rather than just seek employment.

  • Create a vibrant startup ecosystem
  • Develop problem-solving entrepreneurs
  • Bridge academia and industry
  • Contribute to national economic development

Our History

Founded in 2015 by a group of visionary students and faculty members, the PSTU Entrepreneurship Club has grown to become one of the most active student organizations on campus.

What began as a small initiative to promote business ideas among science students has transformed into a vibrant community of over 150 members, supporting numerous startups and hosting regular events.

Our journey has been marked by several milestones, including the establishment of the PSTU Incubation Center in 2018 and our annual Startup Competition that attracts participants from across the region.
